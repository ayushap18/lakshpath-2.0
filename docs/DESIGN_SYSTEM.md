## 1. Brand Identity & Philosophy

### Brand Essence
LakshPath (लक्ष्यपथ — "Path to Your Goal") is an AI career compass for Indian students. The design must communicate:

| Attribute | Expression |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Intelligence** | Clean data visualizations, structured layouts, AI-glow accents |
| **Trust** | Consistent spacing, professional typography, calm color palette |
| **Progress** | Animated progress bars, milestone timelines, streak counters |
| **Accessibility** | Works for Class 10 students AND working professionals |
| **Premium Feel** | Glassmorphism, depth layers, subtle glow effects |
| **Indian Context** | Warm accent tones, Hindi-friendly typography, NSQF/govt scheme awareness |

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity Over Cleverness** — Every element must serve a purpose. No decorative-only animations.
