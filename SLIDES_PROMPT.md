Title: "From Assessment to Job-Ready in 4 Steps"

Step 1: Take 8-question career assessment → Get Career DNA
Step 2: Follow personalized learning roadmap → Track milestones
Step 3: Build portfolio → Get GitHub/LinkedIn scored and improved

- **Demo the app live** — Show the assessment → roadmap → portfolio analysis flow
- **Lead with the problem** — 70% stat is powerful, let it sink in
- **Show the GitHub analysis** — Visual impact of per-repo scoring resonates
- **Highlight the roadmap** — The phase-based milestone view is impressive
- **End with scale** — 100+ endpoints, 24 models shows engineering depth
